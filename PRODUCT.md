# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages (TrendTweekers account). A custom domain will be added later.

## Users

Mac users who arrive from a link on social (Reddit, TikTok, Product Hunt). They are curious, on a phone or a Mac, and decide in seconds whether one of the apps is worth trying.

## Product Purpose

One home for a small indie studio's Mac App Store apps (developer: Peter Hallander). The page shows what each app does by letting the visitor try a tiny piece of it, then sends them to the Mac App Store. Success = a visitor taps through to an App Store listing.

## Positioning

- Private, on your Mac: no accounts, no tracking, nothing uploaded.
- Small, sharp, native: each app does one job well and feels like it belongs on macOS.

## Operating Context

Apps (all macOS 14+, free download with a one-time Pro purchase, no subscription):
- **Clackers: Keyboard Sounds** (ASC 6826136937). Menu bar app. Real recorded mechanical switch and typewriter sounds on every keystroke; pauses during calls; spatial left/right sound. 7 packs (Blue, Silk free; Bright, Brown, Office, 1946, Remington Pro). Pro $5.99. Support: trendtweekers.github.io/clackers-app/
- **Moor: Floating Dock & Launcher** (ASC 6825738474). Floating docks you place anywhere on screen, drag apps/files in, stacks, global shortcuts. Free = 1 dock, 6 items. Pro $14.99. Support: trendtweekers.github.io/moor-app/
- **Lookalike: Duplicate Photos** (ASC 6825814146). Finds duplicate, near-identical and blurry photos in Photos or a folder; keeps the best shot of each set and shows why with a loupe; on-device. Free removes 25 photos. Pro $14.99. Support: trendtweekers.github.io/lookalike-app/

All three were in App Review on 2026-10-23; App Store links (apps.apple.com/app/id<ASC id>) work once approved.

## Product Principles

1. Show, do not tell: a visitor tries each app's core moment on the page.
2. Every claim must be true of the shipped app.
3. Privacy is a fact we state plainly, not a slogan.
4. One clear next step per app: open it in the Mac App Store.
